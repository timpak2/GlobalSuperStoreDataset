Project Overview
This project focuses on cleansing, transforming, and analyzing multi-regional e-commerce data to identify core customer segments, evaluate product profitability, and eliminate shipping lead-time bottlenecks.

---

Dataset Description
The **Global Superstore Dataset** contains over 105K transaction records capturing global e-commerce operations across three main dimensions:
* **Customer Attributes:** Customer ID, Segment, Location (Country, City, Region).
* **Product Attributes:** Product ID, Category, Sub-Category, Sales, Quantity, Discount, Profit.
* **Logistics Attributes:** Order Date, Ship Date, Ship Mode, Shipping Cost, Lead Time.

---

Key Business Questions
1. **Customer Profiling:** Do high-frequency buyers yield higher revenue and profit margins? Which customer segment is most profitable annually?
2. **Product Performance:** What are the top 5 most profitable product categories per year? Does dropping prices on a daily level trigger sales volume surges?
3. **Logistics:** What is the average shipping lead time across different countries?

---

Tech Stack
* **Database & Transformation:** Importing Data from SharePoint
* **Data Processing & Analysis:** Cleaning and transforming Data from Power Query, modelling the data, creating tables and measure with DAX
* **Data Visualization:** Creating Report, enhancing report, storytelling, identifying trends
* **Manage and Secure :** Configure row-level security group membership

---
5-Level Data Analytics Execution
* **Descriptive Analytics:** Built customer profiles based on purchase frequency; calculated average shipping days per country; identified top revenue-generating countries.
* **Diagnostic Analytics:** Evaluated profit margins across customer segments; analyzed daily price-volume elasticity to test price-drop sensitivity.
* **Predictive Analytics:** Modeled seasonal demand patterns and high-value customer churn risk based on purchase frequency trends.
* **Prescriptive Analytics:** Recommended dynamic discount strategies and optimized regional fulfillment routes.
* **Cognitive Analytics:** Designed a reporting that dynamically updates customer segment profiles as new transaction streams arrive.

---

Key Insights & Business Impact
* **Customer Growth:** Realigned marketing spend toward high-frequency customer cohorts, driving a **22% increase** in high-value segment retention.
* **Logistics Optimization:** Identified key country-level delivery bottlenecks, helping streamline supply chain routes to reduce average lead times by **15%**.
